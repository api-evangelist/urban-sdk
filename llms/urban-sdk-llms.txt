# Urban SDK

> Urban SDK is a geospatial AI and data analytics platform for the public sector. It delivers hourly road-speed and traffic data, analytics, and AI tools that help cities, counties, and transportation agencies prioritize issues, pinpoint trends, and act on traffic management, public safety, emergency response, and infrastructure planning — delivered through a web platform (portal.urbansdk.com) rather than a public API.

Urban SDK does not currently publish a public API, developer documentation, or client SDKs. Access to its traffic data and insights is through the Urban SDK web platform and automated Workflows reports.
